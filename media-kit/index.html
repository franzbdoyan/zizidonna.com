Who she is

Zizi Donna is in her forties. She dresses women for the features they have. A strong nose; gray hair; hip dips; a face like their mother’s. Natural fibers over synthetics; clothes kept for a decade. Her videos end with “Prove me wrong.” Her audience, heavy with artists, designers and other creators, takes her up on it in the comments.

Her largest Reels last quarter were on training in one’s forties and on who may look strong. The first drew 693K views and 42.9K interactions. Its title: “Prime in your 20s is a joke. Build strong and grounded in your 40s.” The second drew 1.2M views. Ninety-six percent of her Instagram views come from people who do not yet follow her.

She drives a black Dodge Challenger. The car films cover muscle and sports cars of any make, manual transmissions included. They bring Challenger, Camaro and Mustang owners, and car pages, into a style audience. A woman speaking of muscle cars with taste rather than horsepower has little company there.

Working together

One shoot, several edits, her script. Brands get two rounds of factual corrections. Disclosure is in the first line and spoken on camera, every time.

  • Cross-platform bundle. An Instagram Reel with three Stories frames, a TikTok video of sixty seconds or more, a YouTube Short. The default.
  • Long-form integration. Sixty to ninety seconds inside a YouTube video, found by search for years after.
  • Dedicated video. A full Reel series or TikTok on one product, for partners whose fit is beyond doubt.
  • Automotive series. Driving wardrobe, car care, the road to a car show. Long form first, cut down for Shorts and TikTok.
  • Newsletter placement. One sponsored section in the weekly letter, once the list passes 3,000 readers.
  • Add-ons. Organic usage rights, paid usage and whitelisting, category exclusivity, each priced per thirty days.

The rate depends on the work and on how the film is used. Rate card on request.

Fit

Yes: natural and recycled fibers with published sourcing. Things built to be kept: repairable, resold, serviced. Brands that photograph bodies past forty and strong features. Silver-hair care, skin care at every age, makeup that looks like skin. Cars, car care and driving gear from brands that speak to women as owners.

No: fast fashion and ultra-cheap synthetics. Fillers, injectables, surgery, “anti-aging,” filters. Gray coverage, weight-loss programs, detox, fragranced products. Car-giveaway sweepstakes, predatory financing, cheap parts. Any brief that needs speed or stunts on a public road.
