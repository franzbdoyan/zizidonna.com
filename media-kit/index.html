Who she is

Zizi Donna is a style creator in her 40s who teaches women to dress for the features they have: a strong nose, gray hair, hip dips, a face that looks like their mother's. She argues for natural fibers over synthetics, for keeping clothes a decade, and for an adult relationship with your own appearance. Her videos end with “prove me wrong,” and her audience, heavy with artists, designers and other creators, takes her up on it in the comments.

The throughline is strength. Her biggest Reels of the last quarter were about training in your 40s (“Prime in your 20s is a joke. Build strong and grounded in your 40s”: 693K views, 42.9K interactions) and about who gets to look strong (1.2M views). Ninety-six percent of her Instagram views come from people who do not follow her yet; her reach is discovery, not a fan base.

She also drives a Dodge Challenger, and the lane covers muscle and sports cars of any make, manual transmissions included. Her car Reels pull Challenger, Camaro and Mustang owners and car pages into a style audience, and a woman talking about muscle cars with taste rather than horsepower is almost alone in it.

Fit

She says yes to

  • Natural and recycled fibers with published sourcing.
  • Things built to be kept: repairable, resold, serviced.
  • Brands that photograph 40+ bodies and strong features.
  • Silver-hair care, skin care at every age, makeup that looks like skin.
  • Cars, car care and driving gear from brands that talk to women as owners.

She does not take

  • Fast fashion and ultra-cheap synthetics.
  • Fillers, injectables, surgery, “anti-aging,” filters.
  • Gray coverage, weight-loss programs, detox, fragranced products.
  • Car-giveaway sweepstakes, predatory financing, cheap parts.
  • Any brief that needs speed or stunts on a public road.
