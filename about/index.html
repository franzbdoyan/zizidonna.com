About

I’m Zizi. I live in Bellevue, Washington.

I’m in my forties, with a teenage daughter. My gray hair is kept on purpose. I drive a black Dodge Challenger.

I dress women for the features they have: a strong nose, gray hair, hip dips. Natural fibers when I can find them, and clothes kept for years. No fillers.
